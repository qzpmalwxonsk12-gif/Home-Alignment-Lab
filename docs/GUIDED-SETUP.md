# Home Alignment Lab 0.3.0

## Updating your existing setup

1. Install `Home-Alignment-Lab-0.3.0-measured.apk` and choose **Update**.
2. Open Home Alignment Lab once, then press **Go home and tap 1**. Your existing wallpaper and widget can stay in place.
3. On the yellow widget, tap **1 · Tap here** near its top-left.
4. The app says **Now tap 2 on the widget**. Press its button, then tap the square **2** at the widget's bottom-right. Keep the widget in the same position during both taps.
5. Press **Show pink box**. Check whether all four cyan edges follow the yellow outline. The box stays for 45 seconds.
6. Tap **1** again and choose the answer that matches what you saw.

If it still misses, save the report offered by the app and take a screenshot while the pink box is visible. Attach both in chat. Do not select “it lined up” unless you saw that it did.

If the widget lacks the numbered targets after updating, open the app once to refresh it. If it is too small to tap both targets, enlarge it and start from tap 1.

## First installation

Follow the highlighted button on each screen. First choose the grid or a photo, apply the lab wallpaper through Android's **Set wallpaper / Apply** button, return to the app, and add one widget. The app then guides the same two taps above. Cancelling Android's wallpaper preview does not apply the wallpaper.

The pink box is the initial alignment check. Other home-screen pages and animation are separate later tests.
